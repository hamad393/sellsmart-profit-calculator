SellSmart – E-commerce Profit Calculator

Presets are estimates. Fees vary by country/category/seller tier. You can edit everything.

How this calculator works

  • Selling Price se hum sab costs minus karte hain: Product cost, shipping, packaging, ads, aur platform/payment fees.
  • Platform preset sirf estimated defaults load karta hai — aap apne account ke mutabiq fees edit kar sakte hain.
  • Profit = Selling Price − (All Costs + Fees)
  • Returns ON karne se “expected return loss” add hota hai (Return Rate × Loss per Return).

Formula & Notes
Total Fees = (SP × Platform Fee%) + (SP × Payment Fee%) + Fixed Fees
Total Cost = COGS + Shipping + Packaging + Ads + Total Fees + Expected Return Cost
Profit = SP − Total Cost
Note: Platform fees category/country ke mutabiq change ho sakti hain. Presets estimates hain.
