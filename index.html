Preset fee values are estimates only. Always adjust fees according to your actual marketplace or payment provider account.

How this calculator works

  • This tool calculates your estimated net profit by subtracting all costs and fees from the selling price.
  • Costs may include product cost, shipping, packaging, advertising, and platform or payment processing fees.
  • Platform presets load default estimated fees for convenience and can be edited at any time.
  • Profit is calculated as: Selling Price − Total Costs.
  • If returns are enabled, an expected return loss is added using the selected return rate and loss per return.

Formulas & Important Notes
Total Percentage Fees = Selling Price × (Platform Fee % + Payment Fee %)
Total Fees = Total Percentage Fees + Fixed Fees
Total Cost = Product Cost + Shipping + Packaging + Ads + Total Fees + Expected Return Cost
Profit = Selling Price − Total Cost
Platform fees vary by country, category, seller tier, and payment method. Preset values are estimates only and should not be considered official rates.
